# ElevenLabs Setup 

Go to the ElevenLabs website by typing the following URL in the address bar: elevenlabs.io
On the homepage, look for the "Sign Up" or "Get Started Free" button, typically located in the top-right corner of the page. Click on it.
You will be taken to a registration page. You have multiple options to create your account: Sign up with Google account
Once registration is complete, you will be redirected to the ElevenLabs Dashboard.
